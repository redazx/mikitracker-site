Questions

Good to know

Is Miki free?

Yes, Miki is free to download and use.

Do I need a MyAnimeList account?

No. You can browse what's airing, search the whole database, watch Anime Shorts, read the news and join the community without one. Linking a MyAnimeList account adds your list, stats, achievements, list syncing and the AI features that use your history.

Is Miki the official MyAnimeList app?

No. Miki is an independent app and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by MyAnimeList. It uses MyAnimeList's public API, under its terms, to show and update your lists.

Which phones does it run on?

Miki is built for Android. Other platforms haven't been announced.

Where do the videos and news come from?

Anime Shorts are public videos from official anime channels on YouTube, played in YouTube's own player. News headlines come from Anime News Network and always link back to their site.

How do I contact you, report a bug or delete my data?

Everything is on the Support page — including how to delete your data. Email support@mikitracker.top.

I'd like to advertise in Miki

Great! Email pr@mikitracker.top and we'll tell you how Sponsored videos work. You can also bid for a podium spot on the Featured tab in the app.

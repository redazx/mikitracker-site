Community review

Posts and comments that members have reported. Decide what stays, send a warning that the person sees in the app, or pause their posting. Only for Miki's developer.

Blocked words

Anything containing one of these words or phrases is refused when someone posts. It matches whole words only, so a short word does not catch longer ones.

Keep clears the reports and brings the item back if it was hidden. Delete removes it for good. Warn sends the author your message, shown as a banner in Community and then read in full. Pause posting lets them read but not write for the time you choose, with your message explaining why. An author whose posts are hidden three times in a week is paused for a day automatically; you will see them under "Paused from posting" and can lift it.
